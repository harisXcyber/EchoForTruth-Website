# Conflict Timeline

The nightmare for our Palestinian people began with the 1917 Balfour Declaration, when British Foreign Secretary Arthur Balfour promised our land to create a "Jewish homeland" without consulting those who actually lived there. With these 67 words in a letter, a foreign power decided the fate of our blessed land (Al-Ard Al-Muqaddasa) without any regard for its Muslim and Christian inhabitants who made up over 95% of the population. This colonial decision to give away Muslim lands violated our most basic rights and set the stage for decades of suffering.

Following World War I, Britain took control of Palestine under the Mandate system. During this period (1922-1947), the British facilitated mass Jewish immigration, dramatically altering our homeland's demographics against the wishes of the indigenous population. While European Jews were fleeing persecution, the solution should never have been the displacement of another people from their ancestral homes.

The UN Partition Plan of 1947 proposed dividing Palestine into Jewish and Arab states, allocating 55% of the land to the Jewish population (who owned less than 7% of the land) while Palestinians who owned most of the land would receive only 45%. This unjust proposal was rejected by Palestinians, as it violated their basic right to self-determination on their own land.

Then came the catastrophic Nakba (catastrophe) of 1948, when Zionist forces launched a campaign of ethnic cleansing that resulted in the destruction of over 500 Palestinian villages and the expulsion of more than 750,000 Palestinians from their homes. This was not, as Israeli propaganda claims, a voluntary departure – our people fled in terror after massacres like Deir Yassin where Zionist forces slaughtered over 100 Palestinian villagers including women and children. Those who managed to reach Gaza, the West Bank, or neighboring countries carried their house keys, believing they would soon return, but Israel has denied them this right for over seven decades.

Allah reminds us in the Quran:

"And do not consume one another's wealth unjustly or send it [in bribery] to the rulers in order that they might aid you to consume a portion of the wealth of the people in sin, while you know [it is unlawful]." (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:188)

The theft of Palestinian land and property violated this clear Islamic principle against taking others' possessions unjustly.

In 1967, Israel launched the Six-Day War, occupying what remained of historic Palestine – the West Bank, Gaza Strip, and East Jerusalem – along with Egypt's Sinai Peninsula and Syria's Golan Heights. This occupation brought Al-Aqsa Mosque, our third holiest site in Islam, under Israeli control. Muslims worldwide were shocked and heartbroken as Jerusalem fell under Israeli occupation. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

"A group of my Ummah will continue to fight for the truth and remain triumphant until the Day of Judgment. They will never be harmed by those who oppose them." (Muslim)

This hadith gives us strength to continue resisting occupation of our sacred sites.

Since 1967, we've suffered under Israeli military rule while they built hundreds of illegal settlements on our land. These settlements, housing over 700,000 Israeli settlers in the West Bank and East Jerusalem, violate Article 49 of the Fourth Geneva Convention, which prohibits an occupying power from transferring its own civilians into occupied territory. Each new settlement further fragments Palestinian land, making a viable state increasingly impossible.

Our First Intifada (1987-1993) was a popular uprising against these harsh conditions. Largely nonviolent, it featured mass demonstrations, civil disobedience, and general strikes. Israel responded with brutal force – breaking the bones of Palestinian children, imposing curfews, and collective punishment of entire communities. This uprising led to the deeply flawed Oslo Accords (1993-1995), which created the Palestinian Authority with limited self-governance but failed to end the occupation or stop settlement expansion.

The Second Intifada (2000-2005) erupted after provocative Israeli politician Ariel Sharon, accompanied by hundreds of armed guards, visited Al-Aqsa Mosque compound. This uprising was met with overwhelming Israeli military force, including tanks, helicopter gunships, and F-16 fighter jets used against civilian areas. More than 3,000 Palestinians were killed, and Israel began construction of the "Separation Wall" that snakes deep into Palestinian territory, annexing valuable land and water resources.

In 2005, Israel withdrew its settlements from Gaza but maintained control over its borders, airspace, and territorial waters – transforming it from an occupied territory into what has been called "the world's largest open-air prison." In 2006, Palestinians held democratic elections that resulted in a Hamas victory. Rather than respecting this democratic choice, Israel and Western powers imposed a punishing blockade on Gaza.

Since 2007, this blockade has devastated Gaza's economy and infrastructure, restricting everything from food and medicine to building materials. Israel has launched major military offensives against Gaza in 2008-2009, 2012, 2014, 2021, and the ongoing 2023-2024 campaign – killing thousands of civilians, destroying tens of thousands of homes, and repeatedly targeting schools, hospitals, and essential infrastructure. Each attack has been justified as "self-defense" despite the enormous disparity in casualties and Israel's position as the occupying power.

Recent years have seen further deterioration of Palestinian rights. Israel's 2018 "Nation-State Law" explicitly ensures superior rights for Jewish citizens, codifying apartheid into its basic laws. The Trump administration's so-called "Deal of the Century" and the Abraham Accords encouraged Arab normalization with Israel without securing Palestinian rights. Israel's ongoing ethnic cleansing in East Jerusalem neighborhoods like Sheikh Jarrah and Silwan continues the Nakba into the present day.

Through all this suffering, our steadfastness (sumud) grows stronger, and more people worldwide stand with us. We hold firm to our land with the patience (sabr) that Allah commands:

"O you who have believed, seek help through patience and prayer. Indeed, Allah is with the patient." (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:153)

Despite decades of dispossession and occupation, the Palestinian cause remains alive in our hearts and the hearts of justice-loving people worldwide. This timeline of oppression will eventually give way to a new chapter of liberation and justice, insha'Allah.
