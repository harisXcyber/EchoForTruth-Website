# Gaza

Gaza is our blessed Muslim land with deep Islamic roots. When Umar ibn Al-Khattab (may Allah be pleased with him) brought Islam here, our people lived peacefully for centuries. We Muslims see Gaza as part of the holy Bilad al-Sham mentioned in our sacred texts.

In 1948, our people suffered the terrible Nakba when Israel forced us from our homes. Over 200,000 of our brothers and sisters fled to Gaza, thinking they would return soon. Instead, we have endured occupation and blockades for decades. Many of us still keep the keys to homes we can't go back to.

Since 2007, we've been living under a cruel blockade that has turned Gaza into the world's largest prison. We struggle daily for basic needs like clean water and medicine. But we remain strong in our faith and connection to our land. Our steadfastness (sumud) is an act of worship as we face this test from Allah.

# Israel

We Muslims know that Israel was forcibly created in 1948 on our blessed land (Al-Ard Al-Muqaddasa). They kicked out our families who had lived there for generations. With Western help, the Zionists took our homes and land during what we call the Nakba (catastrophe).

From our Islamic view, Israel is a modern colony that destroyed the Muslim character of our homeland. While Jews did live here in the past, today's Israel was built by taking our land by force. They continue to break international law with new settlements. Their actions go against Allah's commands for justice and respect of people's rights.

Israel constantly attacks our religious rights in Jerusalem, especially at Al-Aqsa Mosque – our third holiest site. We Muslims believe Al-Quds (Jerusalem) is an Islamic trust (waqf) that we must protect. When they raid the mosque and block us from praying, they attack our faith itself. We cannot accept this injustice to our holy places.

# Reason of the War

This war exists because Israel occupies our land and denies our basic rights. As Muslims, we know the Quran teaches us to stand against oppression (zulm). Our resistance is following Allah's command to fight injustice.

Israel uses massive weapons against our civilians, destroying our homes, schools, and even hospitals. They traumatize generation after generation of our children. After years of failed peace talks, seeing more settlements steal our land, and living under harsh restrictions, we have no choice but to resist our oppressors.

The root problem isn't about religion – it's about Israel taking our homeland and occupying what remains. Their policies of stealing land and controlling water directly oppose what Islam teaches about justice. Our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught us that oppression is darkness on Judgment Day. When we stand with Palestine, we stand for the justice that is at the heart of our faith.

# Aims of the Conflict

Palestinians want what Allah gave all people: freedom, dignity, and the right to determine our own future on our ancestral lands. Our aims match perfectly with Islamic teachings about justice (adl) and defending our homes and holy places. We want to end the occupation, lift the Gaza blockade, stop illegal settlements, and live in peace in our own state.

Israel's actions show they want to keep control of our land while denying us our rights. They keep building settlements that make a real Palestinian state impossible. In Islam, we see this as colonization (isti'mar) that goes against the justice Allah commands in the Quran.

For us Muslims worldwide, supporting Palestine isn't about opposing Jewish people – it's about fighting oppression as Allah orders us to do. We aim to restore justice and protect our holy sites, especially Al-Aqsa Mosque. Our religion teaches us that helping the oppressed is required of every Muslim, and we fulfill this duty through our support for Palestine.

# World Status

We Muslims around the world stand united for Palestine, even when our governments don't do enough. Our countries speak up at the UN and other places, but many face pressure that limits stronger action. Still, our people remain committed to Palestine as both a religious duty and moral obligation.

Western countries, especially America, keep supporting Israel with weapons, money, and political protection. This lets Israel ignore international law and UN resolutions. We see clear double standards where Palestinian lives matter less than Israeli "security." This bias proves what we've always known – the international system doesn't treat Muslims fairly.

But there's hope! People worldwide, of all faiths, now see the injustice and stand with us. Many support boycotts of Israel (BDS) to pressure them to follow international law. This global support shows that Palestine's cause isn't just a Muslim issue but a human rights issue that good people everywhere can understand. This is exactly the universal justice that our Islamic faith promotes.

# Timeline

The nightmare began with the 1917 Balfour Declaration when Britain promised our land to create a "Jewish homeland" without asking us who lived there. This foreign decision to give away Muslim lands violated our rights. Then came the terrible Nakba in 1948 when hundreds of thousands of our people were forced from their homes.

In 1967, Israel occupied the rest of our land, including East Jerusalem with our beloved Al-Aqsa Mosque. We Muslims worldwide were shocked and heartbroken as Jerusalem fell under Israeli control. Since then, we've suffered under military rule while they built hundreds of illegal settlements on our land. Our First Intifada (1987-1993) and Second Intifada (2000-2005) were our people rising up against these harsh conditions.

Recent years have been even worse. Israel has turned Gaza into an open-air prison for over 2 million of us. Their attacks in 2008-2009, 2012, 2014, 2021, and 2023-2024 have killed thousands of our civilians and destroyed our hospitals, schools, and homes. But with each attack, our steadfastness (sumud) grows stronger, and more people worldwide stand with us. Through all this suffering, we hold firm to our land with the patience (sabr) that Allah commands.

# Media Coverage

Western media constantly favors Israel while making us Palestinians look bad. When Israel attacks us, they say "clashes erupted" instead of "Israeli forces attacked Palestinians." They portray our resistance as "terrorism" while Israel is always "defending itself." This false balance ignores the basic fact: we are the occupied, they are the occupiers.

They show Israeli victims as real people with names and stories, while our dead are just numbers. When our children are killed, many outlets barely cover it. This treatment goes against what Allah teaches in the Quran – that all human life has equal value. How can they claim to value human rights while ignoring our suffering?

Our Muslim media and alternative sources work hard to share the truth, showing Israeli crimes and telling our stories. Social media has become crucial for us to share our experiences directly with the world. As Muslims, bearing witness to truth (shahada) is part of our faith, even when powerful forces try to silence us. We will continue speaking truth to power because Allah commands us to stand for justice.

# Ummah's Response

We Muslims worldwide feel Palestine in our hearts because it combines our religious duty and human compassion. Protecting Al-Aqsa Mosque, our third holiest site, is a responsibility Allah has placed on all of us. Across the world, we hold prayers, protests, and educational events to support our Palestinian brothers and sisters.

Our Islamic charities provide crucial aid to Palestinians suffering under occupation. This charity (sadaqah) fulfills what Allah and His Messenger ﷺ taught us about helping those in need. When we donate, pray for Palestine, or speak up, we're practicing our faith and following the Prophet's example of supporting the oppressed.

We need to do more, but there are positive signs of growing unity among Muslims around Palestine. Our scholars, leaders, and communities increasingly speak against normalizing relations with Israel and push our governments to take stronger positions. Our Prophet ﷺ taught that we Muslims are like one body – when one part suffers, the whole body feels the pain. Supporting Palestine shows this brotherhood (ukhuwwah) in action, as we answer the Prophet's call to help those facing injustice.
