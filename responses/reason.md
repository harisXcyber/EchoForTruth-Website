# Reason of the War

This war exists because Israel occupies our land and denies our basic rights that Allah has granted to all human beings. As Muslims, we understand that the Quran teaches us to stand firmly against oppression (zulm). Our resistance is not terrorism – it is following Allah's command to fight injustice wherever it exists.

The root causes of this conflict go back decades. Since 1967, Palestinians in Gaza and the West Bank have lived under a brutal military occupation that controls every aspect of their lives. In Gaza specifically, Israel has imposed a suffocating blockade since 2007, restricting everything from food and medicine to building materials and electricity. This blockade has been called "collective punishment" by international organizations – a practice forbidden in Islam, as the Quran teaches us that no soul should bear the burden of another.

Allah says in the Holy Quran:

"And what is [the matter] with you that you fight not in the cause of Allah and [for] the oppressed among men, women, and children who say, 'Our Lord, take us out of this city of oppressive people and appoint for us from Yourself a protector and appoint for us from Yourself a helper.'" (Surah An-Nisa 4:75)

This verse clearly establishes our Islamic duty to stand against oppression and help those who are being oppressed. The Palestinians have endured decades of dispossession, occupation, and humiliation. Their homes have been demolished, their land confiscated, their water resources stolen, and their freedom of movement severely restricted. These conditions make peaceful life impossible and resistance inevitable.

Israel uses massive military force against Palestinian civilians, destroying homes, schools, hospitals, and infrastructure. They have killed thousands of our brothers and sisters, including countless children. These actions violate every principle of proportionality and distinction in warfare that even Western international law requires, let alone the strict ethical guidelines of Islamic rules of combat established by Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and his companions.

The lack of accountability for Israeli crimes encourages further aggression. When Israeli settlers attack Palestinian villages, burn olive trees, or desecrate Al-Aqsa Mosque, they face few consequences. This impunity emboldens extremists and makes peaceful coexistence impossible. Our Prophet ﷺ warned:

"Beware of the supplication of the oppressed, for there is no barrier between it and Allah." (Sahih al-Bukhari)

Years of so-called "peace processes" have failed because Israel has never acted in good faith. While negotiating, they continued building illegal settlements on Palestinian land, making a viable Palestinian state increasingly impossible. These settlements now house over 700,000 Israeli settlers in the West Bank and East Jerusalem, living on land illegally seized from Palestinians. This ongoing land theft reveals Israel's true intentions – not peace, but complete domination of historic Palestine.

The situation in Jerusalem (Al-Quds) is particularly provocative. As Muslims, we hold Al-Aqsa Mosque as our third holiest site after Makkah and Madinah. Yet Israeli forces regularly raid this sacred place, assault worshippers, and allow extremist settlers to perform provocative rituals there in violation of the historical status quo. These attacks on our religious rights and holy sites are a direct assault on our faith and identity as Muslims.

The root problem isn't about religion – it's about Israel taking our homeland and occupying what remains. Their policies of land theft and water control directly contradict Islamic principles of justice and respect for property rights. Our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said in his farewell sermon:

"Verily, your blood, property and honor are sacred to one another."

Yet Palestinians see their blood spilled, property stolen, and honor violated daily under occupation.

The asymmetry of power is stark. Israel possesses one of the world's most powerful militaries, nuclear weapons, and unlimited support from Western powers, particularly the United States. Palestinians have no army, navy, or air force – only their determination to resist oppression and their faith that Allah is with those who stand for justice.

When we stand with Palestine, we stand for the justice that is at the heart of our faith. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught us that "oppression is darkness on the Day of Judgment." Supporting the Palestinian struggle against occupation is not just a political position – it is an Islamic obligation to stand against zulm (oppression) in all its forms.
