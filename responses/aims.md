# Aims of the Conflict

We Palestinians want what Allah has given as a right to all people: freedom, dignity, and the right to self-determination on our ancestral lands. Our aims align perfectly with Islamic teachings about justice (adl) and defending our homes and sacred places. The goals of the Palestinian struggle are clear and reasonable – we seek to end the occupation, lift the Gaza blockade, stop illegal settlements, and live in peace in our own independent state.

The Quran establishes that freedom and human dignity are gifts from Allah to all humanity:

"And We have certainly honored the children of Adam and carried them on the land and sea and provided for them of the good things and preferred them over much of what We have created, with [definite] preference." (Surah Al-Isra 17:70)

This divine honor is not limited to any particular race or religion – it is the birthright of all human beings. Yet Palestinians are denied these basic dignities through military occupation, checkpoints, arbitrary detention, and collective punishment.

Our specific aims include:

1. **End to Occupation**: The complete withdrawal of Israeli military forces from all Palestinian territories occupied in 1967, including the West Bank, Gaza Strip, and East Jerusalem. Occupation is a form of oppression that Islam explicitly condemns, as the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said: "Beware of oppression, for oppression will turn into darkness on the Day of Judgment."

2. **Right of Return**: Implementation of UN Resolution 194, which affirms the right of Palestinian refugees to return to their homes and receive compensation for losses. This aligns with Islamic principles of justice and property rights. The Prophet ﷺ said: "No one takes another's property unlawfully but that Allah will meet him with His anger on the Day of Resurrection."

3. **Jerusalem as Capital**: East Jerusalem (Al-Quds) must be the capital of the Palestinian state, with full Muslim control over Al-Aqsa Mosque. The special status of Jerusalem in Islam makes this non-negotiable. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said regarding Jerusalem: "Journeys should not be undertaken except to three mosques: Al-Masjid Al-Haram (in Makkah), my mosque (in Madinah), and Al-Aqsa Mosque."

4. **Dismantling of Settlements**: All illegal Israeli settlements built on Palestinian land must be dismantled, and the land returned to its rightful owners. These settlements violate international law and Islamic principles regarding usurpation of property (ghasb). The Quran states: "And do not consume one another's wealth unjustly." (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:188)

5. **Sovereign Palestinian State**: Establishment of a fully sovereign, viable Palestinian state with control over its borders, resources, and destiny. This fulfills the Islamic principle that people have the right to govern themselves according to their own values and traditions.

In contrast, Israel's actions reveal their true aims – to maintain control over all historic Palestine while denying Palestinians their basic rights. They continue building settlements that carve up the West Bank into disconnected cantons, making a contiguous Palestinian state impossible. Their "separation wall" snakes deep into Palestinian territory, annexing valuable water resources and fertile land. Their policies in Jerusalem aim to change the city's demographic character through home demolitions, revocation of residency rights, and settlement construction.

For us Muslims worldwide, supporting Palestine is not about opposing Jewish people – it is about opposing oppression, as Allah commands us to do. The Quran states:

"O you who have believed, be persistently standing firm in justice, witnesses for Allah, even if it be against yourselves or parents and relatives." (Surah An-Nisa 4:135)

We aim to restore justice and protect our holy sites, especially Al-Aqsa Mosque. This sacred duty falls on all Muslims, as the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever dies without having fought (for the right cause) or having thought of fighting has died on a branch of hypocrisy."

The Palestinian struggle embodies Islamic values of justice, freedom, and dignity for all people. We seek a solution where Palestinians and Israelis can live side by side in peace – but peace based on justice, not surrender or submission to ongoing oppression. As Muslims, we believe that true peace can only come through justice, as Allah states:

"The word of your Lord has been fulfilled in truth and in justice." (Surah Al-An'am 6:115)

We will continue our legitimate struggle until these aims are achieved – not out of hatred, but out of love for justice and our divinely granted rights to freedom and dignity on our own land.
