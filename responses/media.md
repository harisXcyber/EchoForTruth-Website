# Media Bias & Coverage

Western media constantly favors Israel while making us Palestinians look bad.  
This bias isn’t accidental — it’s systematic.  
It shapes public opinion to support policies that enable ongoing occupation and oppression.

When Israel bombs Gaza, headlines say **“clashes erupted”** or **“violence flares”** instead of telling the truth:  
**“Israeli forces attacked Palestinians.”**

They call our resistance **“terrorism,”** while Israel is always “defending itself.”  
But this false balance hides the reality: **we are the occupied — they are the occupiers.**

---

> **“O you who have believed, if there comes to you a disobedient one with information, investigate,  
> lest you harm a people out of ignorance and become, over what you have done, regretful.”**  
> — *Surah Al-Hujurat (49:6)*

This verse reminds us to verify what we hear,  
especially when the powerful distort truth to serve their agenda.

---

Western media’s language choices reveal deep bias:

- Israelis are **“murdered”** or **“slaughtered.”**  
- Palestinians simply **“die”** or **“lose their lives.”**

Israeli violence is called a **“response”**,  
but Palestinian resistance is immediately labeled **“terrorism.”**  

This framing flips reality on its head —  
as if violence begins with us, and Israel only reacts.  
It erases **75+ years of occupation, blockade, and systemic oppression.**

---

They **humanize Israelis** — with names, faces, families, and stories.  
Our dead are reduced to **numbers without names**.  
Even our children, **killed by airstrikes**, are often ignored in headlines.

> **“Whoever kills a person [unjustly]... it is as though he has killed all mankind.  
> And whoever saves a life, it is as though he had saved all mankind.”**  
> — *Surah Al-Ma’idah (5:32)*

Where is this equal value of life in Western reporting?

---

Most reports **erase historical context**:  
No mention of the **Nakba**, illegal settlements, occupation, or Gaza’s blockade.  
Each outbreak is treated as an isolated event — as if Palestinians resist in a vacuum.

The media portrays this as a **“conflict between equals”** —  
ignoring that:

- Israel is a nuclear-armed military power  
- Palestinians are stateless, blockaded, and defenseless

When Israel drops thousands of bombs on Gaza,  
coverage focuses on **“Israel’s right to defend itself,”**  
not the children buried under the rubble.

They rarely mention Israel’s **Iron Dome**,  
while Palestinians have **no protection** from airstrikes, tanks, and missiles.

---

Most major outlets employ more **Israeli correspondents** than Palestinians.  
This means stories reflect **Israeli fears**, not **Palestinian suffering**.

Journalists who speak up are silenced with **accusations of anti-Semitism**.  
A cynical tactic used to shut down truth.

> **“And do not mix the truth with falsehood or conceal the truth while you know [it].”**  
> — *Surah Al-Baqarah (2:42)*
